Lot Outline Helper for Chrome

The helper puts your finished photos and captions on your HAR listing for you. It works inside your own Chrome, in the Matrix tab you're already signed in to, so we never ask for your MLS login.

The helper is installed in this Chrome. Go back to your order and click Approve upload.

Add it to Chrome

  1. Download the helper: lot-outline-helper.zip. Double-click it to unzip. You'll get a folder called lot-outline-helper.
  2. In Chrome, go to chrome://extensions (type it in the address bar) and turn on Developer mode in the top right corner.
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the lot-outline-helper folder.
  4. Come back to your order and click Approve upload.

This is the early-access install. Once the helper is in the Chrome Web Store it becomes a single "Add to Chrome" click.

What it does, and what it doesn't

It does: open Matrix in a new tab, find your listing, open Manage Photos, upload your Lot Outline photos after any that are already there, and type each caption into its photo's description box.

It doesn't: see or save your MLS password, delete or reorder photos, change any other field, or click Certify and Save. You look everything over and click that yourself.

It only works on lotoutline.com and matrix.harmls.com, and only when you've clicked Approve upload on an order. You can cancel any time from the helper's icon or the panel in Matrix.
